# 43 — Clair de Lune, decoded (slug: clair-de-lune)
Public domain — Debussy (died 1918).
Facts: Claude Debussy, "Clair de lune", third movement of the Suite bergamasque (begun around 1890, published 1905). Key: D flat major. Time signature 9/8. Marked very soft (pianissimo).
Audio: do NOT try to reproduce the exact melody; play a soft, slow Db major texture: Db major (Db F Ab), Bbm, Gb, Ab chords, arpeggiated slowly in the high register, with lots of space.
Facts usable in part 2:
- 9/8 (three groups of three) and very free tempo (rubato) blur the sense of a strong beat — the music seems to float rather than march.
- Five flats (D flat major) means lots of black keys: a soft, rounded sound on the piano.
- Debussy's "impressionism": colour and atmosphere instead of strong tension and release; chords used for their sound, not their pull.
Essence: blur the beat, soften the chords — and music starts to look like moonlight on water.
