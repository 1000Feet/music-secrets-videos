# 35 — Beethoven's Fifth: four notes (slug: beethoven-fifth)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Symphony No. 5 in C minor, Op. 67, premiered in Vienna in 1808. Opening: G G G Eb (short-short-short-long), then F F F D — the same motif a step lower.
Facts usable in part 2:
- A motif: four notes, one rhythm. The rhythm short-short-short-long comes back in all four movements in some form.
- The motif falls a major third (G to Eb), then a minor third (F to D): the two thirds outline the C minor world.
- Legend says Beethoven described it as "fate knocking at the door" — the story comes from his secretary Anton Schindler and many historians doubt it; say it is a legend.
- The symphony travels from C minor to a triumphant C major finale: darkness to light.
Essence: four notes are enough — if you repeat, develop and transform them, they can carry a whole symphony.
