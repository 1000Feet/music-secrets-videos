# 46 — The Blue Danube, decoded (slug: blue-danube)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Johann Strauss II, "An der schönen blauen Donau" (The Blue Danube), waltz, 1866–67. Stanley Kubrick used it for the spaceship scenes in 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968).
Waltz: 3/4 time, "oom-pah-pah": bass on beat 1, chord on beats 2 and 3. Main theme in D major rises on the D major chord: D4 D4 F#4 A4 A4 | (then) A5 A5 F#5 F#5 … (simplified; can be played in D).
Facts usable in part 2:
- Waltz = one strong beat and two light ones: it feels like turning in a circle, which is exactly the dance.
- The melody climbs up the notes of the home chord (an arpeggio) — calm, spacious, opening up.
- In a Viennese waltz the second beat is often played slightly early — a famous little lilt (say "often").
Essence: one heavy beat, two light ones — and the whole room starts to spin.
