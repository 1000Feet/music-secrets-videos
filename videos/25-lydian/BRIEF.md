# 25 — The Lydian Mode (slug: lydian)
Idea: a major scale with a raised fourth. C Lydian = C D E F# G A B.
Songs (part 1) — copyrighted, play scale/chords only:
- The Simpsons theme — Danny Elfman · 1989: built on the Lydian sound (C with F#).
- Flying in a Blue Dream — Joe Satriani · 1989: a Lydian guitar classic.
- Film composers use Lydian for flying, wonder and magic scenes (general statement, no titles).
Why (part 2), all true:
- Raising F to F# removes the half step F → E that normally pulls down to the third; nothing pulls down anymore — it floats.
- The II chord becomes major: C → D major vamp (the D chord contains F#) = the classic "dreamy" Lydian move.
- Lydian is the brightest of the modes: its seven notes are seven perfect fifths stacked from the root (C G D A E B F#).
Essence: raise one note, take away gravity — that's the sound of wonder.
