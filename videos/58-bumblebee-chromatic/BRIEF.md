# 58 — Flight of the Bumblebee: the chromatic scale (slug: bumblebee-chromatic)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov, "Flight of the Bumblebee", an orchestral interlude from his opera The Tale of Tsar Saltan (1899–1900). Famous as a virtuoso showpiece.
Idea: the chromatic scale — all 12 notes, every half step.
Opening idea (can be played, fast sixteenths): E5 Eb5 D5 Db5 C5 Db5 C5 B4 | Bb4 A4 Ab4 G4 F#4 F4 E4 Eb4 … (rapid chromatic descent and buzzing back-and-forth).
Facts usable in part 2:
- Using every half step, with no clear scale, the melody has no "resting" notes — perfect for something that never stops buzzing.
- Very fast, mostly stepwise motion + small back-and-forth turns = the sound of wings.
- The chromatic scale is the circle walked one step at a time (walker visual circling the chromatic circle fast).
Essence: twelve notes, no stops, top speed — and an orchestra turns into an insect.
