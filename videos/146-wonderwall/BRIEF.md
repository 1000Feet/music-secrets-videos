# 146 — The Wonderwall Trick: Notes That Never Move (slug: wonderwall)
Idea: the same two high notes ring through every chord, gluing the progression together.
Songs (part 1) — copyrighted: chords only (generic strumming), no melody:
- Wonderwall — Oasis · 1995. Played with a capo on the 2nd fret, using the chord shapes Em7 – G – Dsus4 – A7sus4.
- On guitar, the ring and little fingers stay on the 3rd fret of the two highest strings for every chord — so the same two top notes ring throughout.
Why (part 2), all true:
- Common tones: notes shared by consecutive chords. Here two notes are shared by all four chords — like a mini drone (see our drone video).
- Only the lower notes change, so the chords seem to move under a fixed ceiling.
- Sus chords (Dsus4, A7sus4) replace the third with the fourth: they sound open and unresolved, so the loop floats.
- Open strings and fixed fingers make it easy to play — and give it a ringing, shimmering sound.
Essence: keep two notes still, move everything underneath — and four chords become one sound.
Visual idea: on the circle, the two shared notes stay lit while the other chord tones move; sus4 tag.
