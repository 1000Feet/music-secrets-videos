# 40 — The Entertainer, decoded (slug: the-entertainer)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Scott Joplin, The Entertainer, 1902, a piano rag. It became hugely popular again after the 1973 film The Sting used it.
Opening pickup (can be played): D4 D#4 E4 C5 E4 C5 E4 C5 — then the main tune in C major with an oom-pah left hand.
Facts usable in part 2:
- Ragtime = a steady, march-like left hand (bass note on the beat, chord on the off-beat: "oom-pah") against a syncopated right hand that lands between the beats — "ragged" time.
- The opening slides up chromatically (D, D#, E) before leaping — a chromatic approach note leaning into the target.
- Joplin wrote "it is never right to play ragtime fast" on some of his rags — a steady tempo makes the syncopation swing.
Essence: a steady heartbeat below, a playful melody that keeps tripping ahead of it — that's ragtime, and the seed of jazz.
