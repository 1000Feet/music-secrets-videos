# 130 — Flamenco's 12-Beat Cycle (slug: flamenco-compas)
Idea: flamenco rhythm (compás) is a 12-beat cycle with accents that switch between threes and twos.
Songs (part 1) — play only the generic compás with palmas (handclaps) and Andalusian-cadence chords (Am G F E):
- Flamenco, from Andalusia in southern Spain, was inscribed by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010.
- Styles like soleá and bulería use the 12-beat compás.
Why (part 2), all true:
- Count 12 beats: accents fall on 3, 6, 8, 10 and 12.
- The first half groups in threes (3 + 3), the second in twos (2 + 2 + 2): it mixes 6/8 and 3/4 — a hemiola built into the cycle.
- Palmas (handclaps), foot stamps and guitar strums (rasgueado) mark the accents.
- The harmony leans on the Phrygian / Andalusian sound: Am – G – F – E (see our Andalusian cadence video).
Essence: twelve beats, accents that shift from threes to twos — and the rhythm burns.
