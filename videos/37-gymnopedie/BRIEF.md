# 37 — Gymnopédie No. 1, decoded (slug: gymnopedie)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Erik Satie, Gymnopédie No. 1, 1888. Marked "Lent et douloureux" (slow and painful). In 3/4. The accompaniment alternates two chords: Gmaj7 (bass G2, chord B3 D4 F#4) and Dmaj7 (bass D2, chord A3 C#4 F#4), one per bar, for the whole opening.
Melody start (can be played): F#5 A5 G5 F#5 C#5 B4 C#5 D5 A4 F#4 (slow, roughly one note per beat with long notes).
Facts usable in part 2:
- Two major seventh chords rocking back and forth, never resolving to a clear home — the music floats.
- Very few notes, lots of space, a slow waltz pulse with no drive: minimalism decades before minimalism.
- The shared note F# stays in both chords, like a thread connecting them.
Essence: two chords, endless space — calm comes from what Satie leaves out.
