# 42 — Eine kleine Nachtmusik, decoded (slug: eine-kleine-nachtmusik)
Public domain — can be played freely.
Facts: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Serenade No. 13 in G major, K. 525, completed in 1787. The title means "a little night music".
Opening (can be played): G4 D4 G4 D4 G4 D4 G4 B4 D5 (the G major chord announced, then rising arpeggio), answered by C5 A4 C5 A4 C5 A4 F#4 A4 D4 (the D7 answer).
Facts usable in part 2:
- The opening simply spells out the home chord (G major), then the dominant chord (D7) answers — a question and an answer built from just two chords.
- A rising arpeggio grabs attention instantly (composers of the time loved this rising "rocket" figure).
- Mozart's style: balanced phrases, clear tonic–dominant harmony, everything in proportion.
Essence: two chords, perfectly balanced, and you can recognise it in two seconds.
