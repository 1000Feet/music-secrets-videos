# 54 — The Leitmotif (slug: leitmotif)
Idea: a short musical idea tied to a character, place or emotion, which returns and changes as the story develops.
Examples (part 1) — do NOT play the copyrighted themes; illustrate with your own short original motifs:
- Richard Wagner's operas (The Ring cycle, first complete performance 1876) are the classic example: dozens of motifs for characters and objects.
- Star Wars (John Williams): characters have their own themes — the Imperial March for Darth Vader.
- Jaws (John Williams, 1975): two notes that mean "the shark is near".
Why (part 2), all true:
- Hearing a motif triggers the memory of the character, even when they aren't on screen — the music tells the story ahead of the picture.
- Change the harmony (major → minor), tempo or instrument and the motif shows how the character changes.
- Demo idea: an original 4-note motif played heroic (major, loud), then sad (minor, slow), then menacing (low, diminished).
Essence: give an idea a melody, and the music can tell you who's coming before they arrive.
