# 21 — Three-Chord Rock: I – IV – V (slug: three-chord-rock)
Idea: the three chords built on notes 1, 4 and 5 of the scale. In C: C, F, G.
Songs (part 1) — copyrighted, play only chords (with a rock beat):
- La Bamba — Ritchie Valens · 1958 · in C: C, F, G.
- Twist and Shout — The Beatles · 1963 · in D: D, G, A.
- Wild Thing — The Troggs · 1966 · in A: A, D, E, D.
- Louie Louie — The Kingsmen · 1963: A, D, Em (a variant: I – IV – v).
Why (part 2), all true:
- C (C E G) + F (F A C) + G (G B D) together contain all seven notes of the C major scale — so these three chords can harmonize almost any simple melody in the key.
- On the circle of fifths, F and G are C's two neighbours: one step each way from home.
- G → C (five to one) is the strongest pull home; F → C is the softer "amen". Rock, blues, folk and country all grew from this.
Essence: three chords, every note of the key — the smallest complete toolkit in music.
