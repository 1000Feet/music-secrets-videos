# 39 — In the Hall of the Mountain King, decoded (slug: mountain-king)

Facts: Edvard Grieg, from the music for Peer Gynt, 1875. Key B minor. The theme repeats over and over, getting faster and louder (accelerando and crescendo) until it explodes.
Theme (can be played): B3 C#4 D4 E4 F#4 D4 F#4 | F4 (E#) C#4 F4 | E4 C4 E4 | B3 C#4 D4 E4 F#4 D4 F#4 B4 A4 F#4 D4 F#4 A4.
Facts usable in part 2:
- The melody creeps up the scale step by step, then sidesteps with chromatic notes (E# and C natural): sneaky, comic menace — trolls tiptoeing.
- No new material is added; repetition + speeding up + getting louder builds unbearable tension.
- Pizzicato low strings and bassoons at the start = tiptoeing; full orchestra at the end = chaos.
Essence: same tune, faster and louder — tension can be built from repetition alone.
