# 55 — The Build-Up and the Drop (slug: the-drop)
Idea: in electronic dance music, the build-up raises tension and the drop releases it.
No specific songs needed (generic EDM); build your own example with chords and drums.
Facts usable, all true and general:
- During the build-up producers often remove the bass and kick, so the ears "miss" the low end.
- A snare roll that keeps doubling its speed (quarters → eighths → sixteenths) and a rising "riser" sound create a feeling of acceleration.
- A pitch or filter sweep upward makes the sound brighter and brighter.
- A short silence (or a single breath) right before the drop makes the return hit harder.
- At the drop the kick and bass come back together on beat one.
- It's the same principle as tension and release in harmony, but built with rhythm, texture and frequency.
Visual idea: grid of 16 cells filling faster and faster; a big "DROP" on the downbeat.
Essence: take something away, make the listener wait, then give it all back at once.
